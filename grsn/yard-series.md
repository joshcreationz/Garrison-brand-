# The Yard Series

**The format:** a real photo of garrison life in Jamaica, **grimey place, pretty picture** (the dirt, zinc, wires and worn walls stay in, but shot with golden-hour light, warm color and a clean composition), with the big white GARRISON wordmark across it. Caption line 1 tells the story of the place. Slide 2 is the piece that matches, then WERUNTINGS.

**Starter ideas:** zinc lane with youths playing ball ("DI LANE WEH WE LEARN FI BALL", World Cup 26 tee); light pole and wires ("WHEN DI LIGHT COME BACK", bandana); dominoes outside a shop ("SATIDEH TINGS", Shower Posse); speaker boxes at dusk ("BEFORE DI DANCE, DI BOXES", Gaza Thug); a painted wall ("EVERY WALL GOT A LEGEND", Mr Wacky); barrels at a gate ("EVERY BARREL GOT A STORY", Coke's American Dream); granny on the veranda ("OUT OF MANY", Coat of Arm hoodie); a route taxi ("MOVE LIKE DI WURL BOSS"); a jerk pan smoking ("SMOKE A RISE", Dudus tee).

## Josh's 8 reference looks
1. **One color in black and white:** B&W yard portrait, only the GARRISON piece in color (from Peter Dean Rickards' Afflicted Yard).
2. **The date stamp:** orange digicam date in the corner, used as the drop date (11 28 26).
3. **POV on the move:** back of a bike, a route taxi window, a fishing boat bow. Reels.
4. **Flash at the dance:** night flash, sound system, real session crowd.
5. **The hustle:** vendor carts, painted jerk carts, downtown stalls. "Every hustle got a uniform."
6. **Places, no people:** pool hall, old Benz on dry grass, painted speaker box in the sun, laundry line over zinc.
7. **Night lanes:** orange streetlight, wordmark glowing white. Teasers.
8. **The archive tape ("Yard Tapes"):** B&W camcorder interviews with real characters, GARRISON logo in the corner like an old TV station bug.

Photographers to study: Peter Dean Rickards, Boogie (take the color and walls, never the guns).
