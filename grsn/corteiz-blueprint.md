## Their marketing playbook
Private Instagram and a password site early; no ads, no seeding, no paid influencers, no interviews; tiny sell-out runs; IRL drops with locations revealed in steps; exchanges with a charity twist; silly-price stunts; a logo with meaning; a drop rhythm (about every 3 months, open 1 to 3 days); co-signs that come naturally.

## Visual DNA
